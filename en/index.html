Client project DIAFAN.CMS

Plast-Rus

Updated design on the existing CMS

Adapted the design for a dealer’s website: responsive homepage, catalog, product pages and price list. Kept its products, prices, contacts and familiar CMS, and restored the logos in SVG.

Open plast-rus.ru ↗

What the client got

  • Easy to use on a phoneResponsive header, menu and product cards. Wide tables scroll inside their container instead of widening the page.

  • Their own data and familiar CMSThe dealer’s products, prices, text and contacts are preserved. The website still runs on the same DIAFAN.CMS.
  • Logos from the original artworkPrepared SVG files for the header and footer, keeping the brand artwork and tagline.

Client project DIAFAN.CMS 5.4

Maxi-Plast

Redesigning a live website without leaving its CMS

I redesigned a greenhouse manufacturer’s website: a responsive homepage, catalog, product pages and price list. The new design is built into the existing DIAFAN.CMS, with no move to another system. The site itself is in Russian.

Open maxi-plast.ru ↗

What the client got

  • Easy to read on a phoneThe old template had no mobile layout: phones showed a shrunk copy of the desktop page.
  • Price and call on the first screenOn a phone, the product page shows the starting price and the “Call” button right away.
  • The same admin panelTexts and prices are edited as before. The client picks the homepage products with a checkbox on the product page.
  • URLs and content keptPages open at the same links; titles and descriptions are unchanged, except for the agreed homepage heading.

FAQ

Common questions

If your question is not here, message me and I will answer.

What do you need from me to start?

Describe the task in your own words: what the result should be and who it is for. Examples, links or your current website help but are not required; we will clarify the details in chat.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. First I look at what it is built with and what needs to change. Sometimes a new design can be built into the existing CMS, as with Maxi-Plast, keeping the same page URLs, content and admin panel. Whether that fits becomes clear after a review.

How are the timeline and price set?

Individually, after we discuss the task. They depend on the scope of the first version, the state of the current project and the integrations needed. I name them before work starts.

What do I get at the end?

The project files and setup instructions. Hosting and further improvements are agreed separately.
