# Plast-Rus portfolio video

Russian case video and its English version, 32 seconds each. Published portfolio assets:

- `plast-rus-case-ru.mp4`: approved 1920×1080 H.264 / AAC video.
- `plast-rus-case-ru-720.mp4`: 1280×720 copy for narrow viewports; same audio stream.
- `plast-rus-poster.webp`: frame from the approved video.
- `plast-rus-case-en.mp4` and `plast-rus-case-en-720.mp4`: English captions and English closing card, with the same continuous audio.
- `plast-rus-poster-en.webp`: English poster frame.

Screenshots of the public website were captured on 11 November 2026. Mobile screens use a 390 px viewport simulation. The phone design and closing card follow the existing Maxi-Plast portfolio video.

Music: “Your Breath” by Eugenio Mininni, [Mixkit Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree). SFX: original Reinh synthesis (`reinh-v2`). The music excerpt runs continuously through the closing card.

Each language page uses its own video and poster. Screenshots show the real Russian-language client website in both versions.
